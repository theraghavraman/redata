Quality principles

Cleaning decisions require domain context, not just a mechanical rule.

Missing ≠ zero

Determine whether a blank means unknown, not applicable, not collected, or a true zero.

Duplicates need context

Repeated customer IDs may be valid across events. Check the key and row grain before deleting.

Types & boundaries

Validate dates, units, allowed categories and logical ranges with business rules.

Statistics desk.

Start with descriptive statistics. Use inference and modeling only when assumptions and study design support them.

Interpretation guide

Numbers become useful only when assumptions and context are clear.

Shape and spread

Mean, median, quartiles, range and standard deviation describe different aspects of a distribution. Inspect outliers and skew.

Uncertainty

Sampling, confidence intervals, tests and effect sizes quantify uncertainty; they do not remove study-design bias.

Evidence for decisions

Define hypotheses in advance. Check randomization, power, multiple testing and practical significance.

Beyond the basics

Methods to learn next, with assumptions stated explicitly.

Confidence intervals

Interval estimates, bootstrap intuition and precision vs confidence.

Correlation & regression
