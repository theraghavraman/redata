Quality principles

Cleaning decisions require domain context, not just a mechanical rule.

COMPLETENESS

Missing ≠ zero

Determine whether a blank means unknown, not applicable, not collected, or a true zero.

UNIQUENESS

Duplicates need context

Repeated customer IDs may be valid across events. Check the key and row grain before deleting.

VALIDITY

Types & boundaries

Validate dates, units, allowed categories and logical ranges with business rules.

Statistical interpretation guide

Numbers become useful only when the assumptions and context are clear.

DESCRIPTIVE

Shape and spread

Mean, median, quartiles, range and standard deviation summarize different aspects of a distribution. Inspect outliers and skew.

INFERENTIAL

Uncertainty

Sampling, confidence intervals, tests and effect sizes help quantify uncertainty; they do not remove bias in the study design.

EXPERIMENTAL

Evidence for decisions

Define the hypothesis in advance. Check randomization, statistical power, multiple testing and practical significance.

Ethics, privacy & reproducibility

Trusted analysis depends on more than a well-written query.

Privacy by design

Minimize personal data, respect purpose limits, apply access controls and document retention.

Reproducibility

Version source data where permitted; document transformations, assumptions, and metric definitions.

Evidence & fairness

Evaluate selection bias, measurement limits, subgroup performance, uncertainty and downstream impact.
